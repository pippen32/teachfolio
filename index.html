Straight answers

Questions, answered honestly.

When does Teachfolio open?

The founding cohort comes first: we are registering the first 20 sellers and seeding the catalog now. Founders hear the opening date first — and no date gets promised until it’s real.

How and when do I get paid?

Sales are processed through Stripe, and your 85% is paid out via Stripe. The exact payout schedule and minimum will be published in writing before opening — no founder uploads a catalog without seeing those rules first.

Are there any other fees?

Membership is $0 and there is no sign-up fee. The platform keeps 15% of each sale, and that is all it keeps. How Stripe’s card-processing fee (2.9% + $0.30) is shared will be announced in writing before launch — no surprises.

Can I keep selling on TpT, Etsy or Gumroad?

Yes. Zero exclusivity means zero exclusivity: this marketplace never asks you to close or pause any other store. (Note the reverse: if you are TpT Exclusive, TpT is the one forbidding you to sell elsewhere — that is exactly the problem this fixes.)

Who sets my prices?

You do. Always. Match your TpT price, go lower, go higher — it’s your store.

Who is behind Teachfolio?

Omar Cruz Padilla, a public-school Spanish teacher in Maryland and resource creator, who is seeding the catalog with his own daily worksheets and his store — founding seller #1 is a teacher too. Not a corporation: a teacher opening the door to other teachers.
